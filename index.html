Quick Answers

Is it legal to sell an inherited gun?

Yes, and it's the cleanest way to be done with it. Selling to a licensed FFL documents the transfer, so the gun's no longer yours.

What do I bring?

Just the firearm and a valid photo ID. You don't need a receipt or any paperwork.

What if it might be loaded?

Leave it exactly as is, put it in your trunk, and call us first. We'll walk you through it, and we handle loaded guns safely at no charge.

Will I get lowballed?

No. We show you recent real sale prices and put the offer in writing. There's no obligation, so you can walk away anytime.

Can you come to me?

For estates and larger collections, yes. We appraise on site and pay you there.

Do you do FFL transfers?

No, we don't handle transfers. We focus on buying, selling, and trading guns.
